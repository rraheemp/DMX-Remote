    Eye-protection zone

    1. Pick Head A or Head B at the top of the page.
    2. Tap New shape, then tap the corners of the area to exclude on the position pad above. The light does not move while you draw.
    3. Tap Save shape (3–12 corners). It is active straight away. Add up to 6 shapes per head.
    1. Tap Start setup. The zone switches off so you can see the beam everywhere.
    2. Pick Head A or Head B at the top of the page.
    3. With someone standing in the crowd area, aim the beam at eye level at the back and tap Mark far edge.
    4. Aim at eye level of the front row and tap Mark near edge.
    5. Repeat 3 and 4 at the left side, centre and right side of the crowd (3–5 positions).
    6. Do the other head, then tap Finish setup. The shaded band on the pad is where the beam goes dark.

    Measure motor speed with the selected head (speed slider at 0): 1 tap Go to start, wait until it stops; 2 tap Go!; 3 tap Arrived the moment it stops.
